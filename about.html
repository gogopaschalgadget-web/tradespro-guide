About TradesPro Guide

Plain guides about plumbing, electrical, heating and cooling, and roofing for homeowners, plus a small store of prompt packs for trade businesses.

Who runs it

One person in Texas, United States. 15 years in the trades, licensed for gas and plumbing. I am also a foundation professional and a drainage expert, a roofing rookie, a previously licensed insurance adjuster and an Eagle Scout.

How the content is made

Pages on this site are written with AI assistance. They are general information, not advice for your specific home. For gas, electrical panel, structural or roof work, hire a licensed professional and follow your local code.

How the site earns money

The site sells digital prompt packs through Gumroad. The site shows no ads right now.

Privacy

No cookies and no tracking of individual visitors. Pages are counted without identifying anyone. The privacy policy has the details.
